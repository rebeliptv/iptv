## What's New

- **Give your favourite team its own channel.** Pick a team in **Settings → Content → Favourite Teams** and it gets a channel of its own, numbered from 4000. Whenever they play — home or away — that channel carries the game, so you can tune straight to your team instead of hunting through tonight's fixtures for them. The rest of the time it shows a card telling you when they're next on, and the guide lists their upcoming games.
  - Adding one takes two clicks: **Add a team**, pick the sport, pick the team. Or just type the name and skip the sport. Add as many as you like, in any sport you follow, and drag them into the order you want their channel numbers in.
  - Your teams then sit on one page as cards, each showing what its channel is doing right now — live, starting soon, or nothing on.
- **Sports events now appear in your lineup before the game, not after it has started.** A game only showed up once its feed was already running, so sports channels were missing from your playlist, your tuner and your guide for most of the day — and a player that reads your lineup once each morning never saw that night's games at all. They now appear as soon as a feed is found, with the start time in the guide.
- **You can now give a custom M3U source its own TV guide.** A guide is picked up automatically when your playlist links one, but plenty of playlists don't — an uploaded file, or a provider that publishes its guide at a separate address — and those channels had no listings and no way to add any. Custom sources now take an **EPG URL** of your own, both when you add one and when you edit one. Leave it empty and nothing changes.
- **Custom channels get listings even when your playlist has no channel ids.** Guide entries used to be matched to your channels by `tvg-id` alone, so a playlist whose lines carry no id — common in a hand-made or trimmed-down M3U — could load a perfectly good guide and show nothing from it. Channels are now matched by name as well.
- **The sources list tells you whether the guide actually worked.** Each custom source shows how many guide entries it holds, and when it holds none it says why: the playlist links no guide, the guide couldn't be downloaded, or the guide covers none of your channels. All three used to look identical — a green source, working channels, and an empty guide with nothing to explain it.
- **Fewer needless switches away from a good feed.** Your server keeps an eye on how well each feed is keeping up and moves you off one that has fallen behind. It was judging that on the feed's whole history, so a single dropped moment hours earlier counted against a feed that had been perfect ever since — and switching away usually meant landing on a worse one. It now judges a feed on how it is doing right now, and a long clean run clears an older black mark.
- **Your TV guide no longer comes back empty after a database hiccup.** A momentary database problem produced a guide that was perfectly valid and completely empty, and your player kept it until the next refresh. Your server now returns an error your player simply retries.
- **Running behind a reverse proxy is now properly supported.** A new `TRUSTED_PROXIES` setting tells your server which proxy addresses to trust for visitor IP addresses. By default it trusts none — which also means nobody can dodge the dashboard's login rate limit by faking one.
- **A stuck refresh can no longer wedge the server.** Refreshing just your channels, your events or your guide now gives up after 20 minutes, the same as a full refresh. One that hung used to block every later refresh until you restarted the container.
- **Your pinned ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX channels always sort to the top of your lineup.** One could briefly appear further down instead.
- **A pile of dashboard fixes.** Long channel names truncate instead of overflowing, short programmes in the guide no longer overlap their neighbours, the guide stops jumping back to the top while it refreshes in the background, a failed action shows an error instead of looking like it worked, every dialog closes with Escape, and the channel grid and play buttons work fully from the keyboard.
- **Custom source addresses are checked as you edit them.** Editing a source's address now gets the same check as adding one, so a typo is a clear message straight away instead of a confusing error later.

## Update

Already running Rebel IPTV? Update to this version — your data (the `iptv-data` volume) is kept. From the dashboard, click **Update now** on the Dashboard or in **Settings → Server** (on the `:latest` image with the Docker socket mounted). Or from the command line:

```bash
docker pull rebeliptv/iptv:1.3.8
docker compose up -d
```

Setting up a **new** instance? The step-by-step guide at **https://www.rebeliptv.com** builds a Docker configuration for your system — ports, timezone, in-app updates, and more — so you copy a setup that fits your box instead of a generic one.

Image: `rebeliptv/iptv:1.3.8` (also published as `rebeliptv/iptv:latest`).

## Endpoints

| URL | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `http://<server>:8080/playlist?key=YOUR_KEY` | M3U playlist |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (XML) |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg.gz?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (gzip) |
| `http://<server>:8080` | Web dashboard |

> **Note:** The `?key=` parameter is only required if an API key has been generated in the web dashboard under **Settings**.
