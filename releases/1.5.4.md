## What's New

Back up and restore your server, choose the channels you want from your custom playlists, and keep this morning's guide when your server's time zone is ahead of UTC, along with security and other fixes across your server and the dashboard.

#### Your server

- **Back up your server and restore it.** Settings → System → Backup → Download a backup saves your lineup, settings, sports picks, custom playlists and own guide in one file. Restore from a backup shows what the file would change, with a switch for each part, so you can put everything back after a reinstall or copy just your lineup to a second server. A backup holds no sign-ins or keys, so after a restore your server lists anything left to do by hand, such as connecting Jellyfin again. The setup wizard can restore a backup too.
- **Your guide keeps this morning's programmes when your server's time zone is ahead of UTC.** On a server in Europe, Asia or Australia, each refresh deleted the day's listings up to as many hours past midnight as the zone is ahead of UTC (until 11:00 in Sydney), the programme on now included.
- **A restart keeps your files and your latest changes.** A `.json` file you kept in your server's data folder, such as a backup, was deleted every time your server started, and with a channel playing, a restart could end before your server saved your latest changes.
- **Your server's Docker healthcheck works when you set the `HOST` environment variable to one address.** `HOST` is the address your server listens on, every address unless you set it. With it set to one address, Docker marked a working server unhealthy. An update that can't replace the old container now starts it again, rather than leave your server stopped.

#### Custom playlists and your own guide

- **Choose which channels of a custom playlist you keep.** Settings → Sources → Custom playlists → Choose channels lists a playlist's channels in its own groups, each with a switch. A channel that's off is left out of your playlist, guide and tuner and gives up its number, and turning a whole group off also keeps off the channels your provider adds to it later.
- **Custom channels keep their ids when your provider reorders or adds to a playlist.** With this update they move to new ids, which start with the playlist's name. Refresh the guide in Jellyfin or Emby after you update, and they list your custom channels once as new channels. In Plex, match them to their guide listings again, unless you connected Plex under Settings → Players → Media servers, which does it for you.
- **Your custom playlists keep their channel numbers and guides through a refresh.** A player that read your lineup partway through a refresh could find two channels on one number, or a playlist's channels with nothing on. A guide over 64 MB now says it's too large, and deleting a playlist removes its uploaded guide.
- **Your own guide's artwork stays in the guide.** Pictures your own guide gives its listings were cleared, or replaced with a film poster, within half an hour of loading it.

#### Channels and playback

- **A game tuned in its pregame hour, before its stream is ready, shows its Upcoming screen.** Plex, Emby, Jellyfin and the dashboard showed nothing at all until the app gave up.
- **A channel whose sound your server converts for Plex, Emby or Jellyfin keeps its sound after a damaged moment in its audio.** It lost its sound, or went muffled, for the rest of the show.
- **A channel keeps to its own streams.** When its stream failed, a channel could fall back to another one listed under its name: a foreign-language version, a local station's 24/7 news stream in place of the station, or a sitcom marathon for a station whose call sign is in the title. These stay off the channel now, and a few channels gain streams they were missing.

#### The dashboard

- **Fixes across the dashboard.** The Guide catches up after midnight and follows a lineup you've just saved, pages say they're loading rather than "not found", the player shows what's on for your custom playlists' channels, and the setup wizard keeps your channel choices however you move past their step. Event pages, Settings and signing in get smaller fixes.

#### Security

- **A web page you visit can no longer start a refresh or an update on your server.** Nor can a custom playlist's logo or a crafted link make the dashboard start one.
- **A broken or hostile source can no longer run your server out of memory or keep it busy.** A guide that unzips without end, a playlist of endless empty lines, or a stream whose audio never ends or keeps skipping ahead is refused or let go rather than held.
- **Tighter sign-in and links.** Two first-run setups at once make one admin, your sign-in cookie is marked Secure behind an https proxy, a channel's copied stream link no longer includes your playlist key, a name from a source can no longer add a line to your playlist, and the dashboard runs only its own scripts.

## Update

Already running Rebel IPTV? Update to this version — your data (the `iptv-data` volume) is kept. From the dashboard, click **Update now** on the Dashboard or in **Settings → System** (on the `:latest` image with the Docker socket mounted). Or from the command line:

```bash
docker pull rebeliptv/iptv:latest
docker compose up -d
```

On an image pinned to a version, change its tag in your compose file to `:1.5.4` and run `docker compose up -d`.

Setting up a **new** instance? The step-by-step guide at **https://www.rebeliptv.com** builds a Docker configuration for your system — ports, timezone, in-app updates, and more — so you copy a setup that fits your box instead of a generic one.

Image: `rebeliptv/iptv:1.5.4` (also published as `rebeliptv/iptv:latest`).

## Endpoints

| URL | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `http://<server>:8080/playlist?key=YOUR_KEY` | M3U playlist |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (XML) |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg.gz?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (gzip) |
| `http://<server>:8080` | Web dashboard |

> **Note:** The `?key=` parameter is only required if an API key has been generated in the web dashboard under **Settings → Security**.
