## What's New

A big release: a new video player in the dashboard with closed captions, custom sources that read files from your server, channels that stay on in Jellyfin and Plex while they wait, and a long list of playback and reliability fixes.

#### New

- **A new video player in the dashboard.** It has its own controls, the same in every browser: what's on across the top, a timeline you can skip back and forth through what's buffered, a **LIVE** button that shows how far behind live you are, volume, picture-in-picture and fullscreen. Keyboard: Space or K to play and pause, ← → to skip 10 seconds, ↑ ↓ for volume, M to mute, C for captions, F for fullscreen, P for picture-in-picture, End to jump to live.
- **Closed captions.** Channels that carry captions can show them in the dashboard player: turn them on from the **CC** button (CC1 is the usual choice). They start off on every channel.
- **Custom sources can read files from your data folder.** Put a playlist in the `custom-m3u` folder or an XMLTV guide in the `custom-epg` folder of your data folder (`/app/data`), and pick it under **Settings → Sources → Custom M3U Sources**. It's read again on every refresh, so a guide another program keeps writing stays current. If `/app/data` is a Docker volume (the default setup), mount a folder from your server at `/app/data/custom-m3u` or `/app/data/custom-epg` instead; the dashboard shows the line to add.
- **A redesigned Custom M3U Sources section.** One table with an on/off switch for each source, one window to add or edit a source, and its guide as a single choice: the playlist's own, a URL, an uploaded file, or a file from your data folder.
- **Two new local markets: Buffalo, NY and Charleston–Huntington, WV.** Choose one under **Settings → Content → Local Market** to get its ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX stations, for the live feed and the guide.
- **More in the dashboard.** Each Multiview screen has a **Refresh** button, and with stream stats on, a channel's page shows them whenever the channel is playing anywhere, in Jellyfin, Plex or another app.

#### Jellyfin, Plex and other apps

- **Channels stay on while they wait.** Since 1.4.7, a channel that took a few seconds to start showed the "Connecting to your channel" card and then closed: Jellyfin went back to the guide and Plex ended the session. The card now stays for as long as the wait lasts (starting, reconnecting, at your stream limit, or before a game) and the channel comes on by itself.
- **A channel uses one of your simultaneous streams only once it plays.** A channel still starting, or trying sources that don't work, no longer counts, and a TV left on a channel that went dark no longer holds a stream forever.
- **Audio-only channels keep playing after the "Connecting" card** instead of going silent.
- **HDHomeRun tuner fixes.** Channels that share a number, or have none, now get their guide info; channel numbers in the tuner lineup no longer go stale; and custom channels tune without exposing their source's address on your network.
- **Players that load the guide from your playlist get it with an API key set.**

#### Playback

- **Faster, more reliable starts.** Channels and games go straight to their best working source, reuse a link that's still good instead of fetching a new one every time (which took over half a minute on some channels), try every stream server a game has, and get their full start-up time when their other sources are busy.
- **Fewer freezes, skips and jumps.** Channels no longer jump back and replay a second every minute, freeze for a minute and a half when a source stops sending, freeze when an encrypted feed changes its key, stutter across ad breaks, or skip a moment between programmes. Game streams no longer skip a few seconds every minute, and channels left on for more than a day stay smooth.
- **Smoother switches to a backup source.** No more 15-second blackout when the backup's sound is in another format, no needless reconnects when sources differ only in audio bitrate, and no silent tuner after a switch. A backup that's slow to start gets a fair chance, and one that keeps dying is set aside.
- **Channels start at live.** A feed that keeps a long rewind window could start minutes or hours behind live; every channel now starts just your buffer's depth behind.
- **More streams play.** Some broadcast-style streams that never showed a picture now play, channels that only play through one of their source's alternative players start again, and a fault in one stream can no longer stop every stream on your server.
- **Lighter streams.** Live channels use about 6% less bandwidth, and your server uses less memory while you watch.

#### Channels

- **Online means it plays.** A channel shows online only once a check has confirmed one of its sources works, and goes offline as soon as none does. A source that sends an empty stream, has lost its sound or uses a format your server can't deliver is caught and skipped for one that works.
- **Fewer false offlines.** A restart, a slow or interrupted check, a source briefly refusing a connection, a changed or redirecting link, or a refreshed link that turned out broken no longer takes a working channel offline. The health check also no longer competes with your viewers for a source's connections.
- **More backup sources.** Over 170 local stations and more UK and Canadian channels gained a source, and sources whose login contains characters such as `!`, `#` or a space play again.
- **The right channel, everywhere.** A channel no longer plays another country's version of itself, channels dropped from the lineup stay dropped, and a channel that plays shows in your playlist and tuner lineup, not just the guide.
- **ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX follow your local station,** even when you've hidden it: they go offline with it and come back with it.
- **Sources that move are followed without a restart,** including a site that briefly shows a maintenance page.
- **Custom sources load and stay right.** Large or slow playlists get up to three minutes to download, every version of a channel (FHD, HD, SD) gets its guide, and a deleted source stays deleted, even mid-load or while your server is busy.

#### Sports

- **Games stay on through their postgame.** Your team's channel and the game's own channel carry the game until its postgame ends, including after overtime, extra innings or a rain delay, instead of stopping at the final whistle.
- **The first game of a doubleheader plays.** The channel follows the game being played, then moves on to the next one.
- **Games find the right streams.** A team listed by a short name is matched to the right game, a shared stream is no longer played before a game starts, newly found streams aren't lost between refreshes, a dead stream no longer shows as working, and a game no longer drops out of your lineup when one source fails to load. An oddly spelled event name from the Rebel relay no longer stops sports updating.
- **Tidier sports pages.** Finished games leave the dashboard's Live & Upcoming list, a game is offered to play only where it has a working stream (the same on every page), and football and basketball pages no longer show a live drive after the final, a repeated last play or stale stats rows.

#### Guide

- **No more doubled-up programmes.** A programme whose start time changed no longer shows twice in your players' guides.
- **ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX show one schedule everywhere:** the network's own when the guide has one, otherwise your local station's.
- **A better dashboard Guide.** It shows your custom channels, opens even during a refresh, and shows its whole last evening on servers behind UTC.
- **Movie posters survive a busy moment upstream,** and **Refresh guide** refreshes the Rebel relay's guide on servers using it.

#### Dashboard

- **A steadier player.** A paused channel picks up at live instead of freezing (and stops downloading after 10 minutes paused), the first picture shows sooner, a frozen picture recovers on its own, mute stays on through reconnects, and moving between pages no longer leaves the player stuck.
- **Error messages in your language.** Errors, sign-in and setup messages and update hints now follow the dashboard language.
- **Regenerating or deleting the API key asks first,** since either one breaks every link that uses the old key.
- **Settings that behave.** Refresh buttons no longer stick on "Refreshing…", the sports switches apply at once, clearing a number field no longer saves a limit of 1, a cancelled drag puts the order back, and the setup wizard keeps a country on and notices when your channels arrive.
- **Accurate numbers.** Watch time counts only what was watched, the guide coverage figure no longer goes past 100%, counts no longer flash to 0 during a refresh, and stream stats show your own buffer for as long as you watch.
- **And more:** the dashboard loads faster, the Channel Lineup page lists channels in your players' order, Multiview's picker shows the games on now, and Arabic and Urdu layouts mirror properly.

#### Your server

- **Your data is safer.** If your server can't read its saved data at startup, it now keeps it and tells you why instead of starting over, and saving recovers once you free space on a full disk. Saving and loading use far less memory, and the log warns you when the data folder can't be written.
- **More reliable in-app updates.** Updates keep working after a rolled-back update, keep your data when nothing is mounted at `/app/data`, and work with rootless Docker or any other Docker socket path.
- **Your server recovers on its own** from a badly timed network outage and from an unexpected error in a background job, and a server on the Rebel relay no longer rebuilds its channels unexpectedly after a restart.
- **Your API key and settings stay saved** through a refresh or a busy moment.
- **The `LEAGUES` environment variable carries over on upgrade again,** once; after that, choose leagues under **Settings → Content → Sports**.
- **Lighter on your hardware.** Refreshes, guide downloads and large custom playlists use far less CPU and memory, and memory no longer creeps up with every refresh, which helps most on a NAS or a Raspberry Pi.

## Update

Already running Rebel IPTV? Update to this version — your data (the `iptv-data` volume) is kept. From the dashboard, click **Update now** on the Dashboard or in **Settings → Server** (on the `:latest` image with the Docker socket mounted). Or from the command line:

```bash
docker pull rebeliptv/iptv:1.4.8
docker compose up -d
```

Setting up a **new** instance? The step-by-step guide at **https://www.rebeliptv.com** builds a Docker configuration for your system — ports, timezone, in-app updates, and more — so you copy a setup that fits your box instead of a generic one.

## Endpoints

| URL | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `http://<server>:8080/playlist?key=YOUR_KEY` | M3U playlist |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (XML) |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg.gz?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (gzip) |
| `http://<server>:8080` | Web dashboard |

> **Note:** The `?key=` parameter is only required if an API key has been generated in the web dashboard under **Settings**.
