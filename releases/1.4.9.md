## What's New

A new dashboard and Settings page, game pages rebuilt for every sport with scores that don't spoil the game, channel numbers that start where you want, and fixes for channels that stutter or are slow to start.

#### New

- **A new dashboard.** The dashboard puts what's on beside how your server is running. Under its title, one line says how your server is doing, then anything that needs you, then four numbers that open their pages: who's watching, your channels, the games and what's been watched today. On the left are what's playing now, the next games and your most-watched channels, each with a Play button, and every category and league to browse. On the right are the day's watch time hour by hour, how reliably your streams played, and your playlist and guide addresses to copy. Nothing jumps when someone starts or stops watching, and on a phone it's one column.
- **A new Settings page.** Settings has eight sections, each answering one question: Overview, Channels, Sports, Sources, Players, Streaming, Security and System. It opens on an overview of how your server is doing, anything that needs you and your instance ID, a search box finds any setting, and every change says when it's saved. Bigger changes ask first: switching the feed source, renumbering your channels, turning sports off, turning the HDHomeRun tuner on and adding the first dashboard account. Channel numbering is now under Channels, simultaneous streams under Streaming and the language under System. On a phone, the section picker keeps the badges that flag what needs a look.
- **The Rebel IPTV logo.** The dashboard wears the same logo and icons as rebeliptv.com: in the menu bar, on the sign-in page, in the setup wizard and on the browser tab, and as the icon when you add the dashboard to a phone's home screen.
- **Click a show that's on to open its channel.** In the **Guide**, clicking a show that's on now opens its channel, as clicking the channel's name does. Shows later in the day open nothing, since their channel is playing something else until then.
- **Start your channel numbers where you want.** A new **Channel number offset** under **Settings → Channels** adds the same amount to every channel number in your playlist, guide and HDHomeRun tuner, so Rebel IPTV's channels stay clear of another tuner's in Plex or Jellyfin. With an offset of 100, channel 1 becomes 101. Changing it renumbers every channel, so rescan the tuner in your player afterwards.
- **Five more home markets.** Atlanta, Columbus, Madison, Providence and Toledo join **Settings → Channels → Local stations**, so ABC, CBS, NBC and FOX can play those cities' own stations.

#### Sports

- **Game pages rebuilt for every sport.** A game's page shows the scoring plays and key moments, each team's on its own side of the timeline, the score by period, team and player stats and, in football, every drive. Before a game it shows the probable starters, recent form, this season's earlier meetings and injuries, and around it the line-ups, standings, officials and weather, where the league has them. Our own three stars and the story of the game (a comeback, lead changes, a hat-trick, a shutout, an overtime winner, a walk-off) are worked out for you. Fight cards show every bout and how it ended, races each session's results, and golf the leaderboard. Game status and stat names appear in your dashboard's language, and betting odds are no longer shown.
- **Every sport shows the same numbers.** Before a game, its page compares both teams' season so far. Soccer pages now list every player's line, goalkeepers apart, and the game's top performers, and baseball pages compare the teams' hitting and fielding, as the other sports' pages do. Football pages add the defense, kicking, punting and return tables and a fuller team comparison, and keep each team's recent form once the game starts.
- **Scores that don't spoil the game.** Sports pages can hold scores, plays and stats back to match your video, so a goal doesn't appear before you see it. Match my video is on by default and works out the delay from your server's own buffering; choose Live scores or a fixed delay in **Settings → Sports**. The choice is kept per browser. A game's page shows its stats as soon as it opens.
- **Live games keep up.** During a game its page now refreshes every 5 seconds and the sports list every 15, and both show scores and plays sooner, while your server asks for sports updates far less often to do it. A suspended game no longer shows as live; it resumes another day.

#### Channels and playback

- **A channel moves off a feed that keeps cutting out.** Since 1.4.8, a channel whose feed kept cutting out every few seconds stayed on it, stuttering, and never tried its other feeds. It now gives that feed up after a few tries and plays another.
- **Channels that are slow to start open cleanly in the dashboard.** In the dashboard's player, a channel that started just after the Connecting card went up failed its first try and had to start again, and the card itself took most of a second to appear. The card now shows at once, and the channel follows it.
- **Channel logos stay current in your player.** Plex and Jellyfin keep a channel's logo until its address changes, so a new logo never reached them; now it does. A channel with no logo shows its name instead of a broken image.
- **Saving a custom source no longer fails while your server is busy.** Adding or editing a source, switching it on or off, or uploading its file could fail with a server error while your server was saving something else, such as a refresh. It now waits its turn.
- **A custom source that points back at your server is removed.** A custom source whose playlist was your server's own playlist made it load its own channels again, in a loop. Your server now removes such a source, with the copies of your channels it made, as soon as it starts, and drops an EPG URL that is your server's own guide.

#### Your server

- **Your server has its own identity key.** It makes an `instance.key` file in its data folder (`/app/data`) on its first start and signs in with it. Keep it with the rest of the folder when you back up or move your server. Your instance ID stays the same.
- **Your server cleans up after itself.** Files left in your data folder by a stop partway through a guide download, an upload or a save are deleted the next time your server starts. A copy of saved data it couldn't read, which it keeps in case you need it back, is now deleted after 30 days.

## Update

Already running Rebel IPTV? Update to this version — your data (the `iptv-data` volume) is kept. From the dashboard, click **Update now** on the Dashboard or in **Settings → Server** (on the `:latest` image with the Docker socket mounted). Or from the command line:

```bash
docker pull rebeliptv/iptv:1.4.9
docker compose up -d
```

Setting up a **new** instance? The step-by-step guide at **https://www.rebeliptv.com** builds a Docker configuration for your system — ports, timezone, in-app updates, and more — so you copy a setup that fits your box instead of a generic one.

Image: `rebeliptv/iptv:1.4.9` (also published as `rebeliptv/iptv:latest`).

## Endpoints

| URL | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `http://<server>:8080/playlist?key=YOUR_KEY` | M3U playlist |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (XML) |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg.gz?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (gzip) |
| `http://<server>:8080` | Web dashboard |

> **Note:** The `?key=` parameter is only required if an API key has been generated in the web dashboard under **Settings**.
