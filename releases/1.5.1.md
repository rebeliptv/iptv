## What's New

Build your lineup yourself, in groups of your own if you like, connect Jellyfin, Emby or Plex from the dashboard, and put your sports at the top of your apps' guides.

#### Your lineup

- **Build your own lineup.** Settings → Channels → Manage lineup is now two lists: your lineup on the left and every channel on offer on the right. Drag channels across to add them where you drop them, or Add all from what the filters show. Within your lineup, drag a channel or type its number to move it, and drag it back or press × to take it out. Tick several to move or take them out together. Arrange sorts your lineup A to Z, by category or by country, and Undo takes back your last change. Your changes wait until you press Save, then reach your playlist, guide and media servers all at once; Discard puts back what you last saved, and leaving with changes unsaved asks first. On a phone the two lists are tabs.
- **Group your channels your way.** Settings → Channels → Group channels is now None, By country or My own groups. By country runs your lineup country by country until you arrange it yourself; then your order stays, and your playlist still groups channels by country. With My own groups, you make groups such as Locals, News or Sports: add, rename, collapse and move them, drag channels between them, or tick some and pick Move to group. Give a group a starting number and its channels keep their numbers when a group above it changes. Your playlist groups channels under your group names. Switching to it starts from your lineup as it is, so nothing moves.
- **Lineup actions.** Under Actions: Take all out, Reset order (back to the default order), A group per country (with My own groups), and Reset to default, which puts every channel back in, in the default order for your settings. Each asks first, and Undo puts your lineup back. The two resets save straight away.
- **New channels wait once your lineup is your own.** After you've changed your lineup, a newly offered channel waits in All channels, marked New, until you add it, and your dashboard tells you when some arrive. A lineup you haven't changed still gets new channels straight away.
- **Five-digit channel numbers fit in the Guide and on the lineup page.** A channel number of five digits, such as your own playlists' from 10000, was split over two lines. It now fits on one.

#### Media servers

- **Connect Jellyfin, Emby or Plex.** Under Settings → Players → Media servers, paste an API key from your Jellyfin or Emby and your server sets itself up there: its tuner, its guide and the options they need. A tuner you already added is taken over in place, so your favourites and recordings stay. Name it as you want its tuner and guide to show there: it's Rebel IPTV unless you change it. Your server keeps it all that way, and the guide there refreshes straight away after each refresh here, instead of up to a day later, and each time you save your lineup. Emby's Live TV needs Emby Premiere. Plex works through this server's HDHomeRun tuner and needs Plex Pass: paste your X-Plex-Token where the others take an API key.
- **The setup wizard finds your media server.** Its Media server step lists the Jellyfin, Emby and Plex it can reach on this computer and your network, so connecting one takes only its key. One it doesn't find you connect by its address, and any other app you set up by hand, as before. A media server in a container on the same Docker network, found there or connected in Settings, is set up to reach this server by its container name, such as `http://iptv:8080`, rather than the address you opened the dashboard at.

#### Sports

- **Sports first.** Turn on **Sports first** under Settings → Channels → Numbering, and Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and other apps that sort by channel number list your team channels, league channels and games ahead of your other channels, at the top of the guide instead of the bottom. The sports keep their numbers, and your other channels move up past them: channel 1 becomes 10001. Rescan the tuner in your app after you change it.
- **Basketball previews compare more of each team's season.** Before a game, Season so far lists each team's shooting percentages and its rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and turnovers a game beside its points. A team that hasn't played yet this season reads a dash for its recent form.

#### Channels and playback

- **The Connecting card no longer smears as your channel comes in.** In Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and other apps, your channel took over from the Connecting card partway through one of its pictures, so your app could show the card with its lower half smeared until the channel played. The card now finishes its picture first.
- **Fewer Reconnecting cards on games.** Some games' streams list each new piece of video a few seconds before they can send it. Your server waited 2 seconds for a late piece and then skipped it, and every skip took seconds out of your buffer for good, until the Reconnecting card came up. It now waits as long as the piece lasts, usually 4 to 6 seconds.
- **A game's backup streams are different streams.** When a game's first stream came up before its others, every backup your server held for the game was a copy of it until the next refresh, so when it dropped they all did, and you sat on the Reconnecting card. They now move onto the game's other streams as those come up.

#### Your server

- **A full disk now shows in your server's log.** When the disk your server keeps its data on fills up, its channel list and TV guide stop updating. The container's log now says so once, with what to free.
- **The dashboard catches a container hostname Docker made up.** If your container's hostname is the ID Docker gave it, Settings → Players says so and keeps it out of your playlist and guide addresses, which would stop working the next time the container is recreated. Set `hostname:` or `container_name:` in your compose file, or `SERVICE_NAME`, which now works.

## Update

Already running Rebel IPTV? Update to this version — your data (the `iptv-data` volume) is kept. From the dashboard, click **Update now** on the Dashboard or in **Settings → System** (on the `:latest` image with the Docker socket mounted). Or from the command line:

```bash
docker pull rebeliptv/iptv:latest
docker compose up -d
```

On an image pinned to a version, change its tag in your compose file to `:1.5.1` and run `docker compose up -d`.

Setting up a **new** instance? The step-by-step guide at **https://www.rebeliptv.com** builds a Docker configuration for your system — ports, timezone, in-app updates, and more — so you copy a setup that fits your box instead of a generic one.

Image: `rebeliptv/iptv:1.5.1` (also published as `rebeliptv/iptv:latest`).

## Endpoints

| URL | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| `http://<server>:8080/playlist?key=YOUR_KEY` | M3U playlist |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (XML) |
| `http://<server>:8080/epg.gz?key=YOUR_KEY` | EPG guide (gzip) |
| `http://<server>:8080` | Web dashboard |

> **Note:** The `?key=` parameter is only required if an API key has been generated in the web dashboard under **Settings → Security**.
